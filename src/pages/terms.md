Abii's control logic is designed with that constraint in mind, but **you are responsible for the condition of your own HVAC system.** If your ductwork or blower is already undersized, damaged, or marginal, tell us during beta setup. If you notice unusual noise, reduced airflow, short cycling, or any other change in how your system runs after installing Abii, remove the Abii vents and consult an HVAC professional.
