## Our commitment

Abii is meant to make homes more comfortable for everyone in them. That includes this website. We build to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**.

## What we've done

- Every page works with the keyboard alone, and focus is always visible
- Headings follow a real document outline rather than being chosen for size
- Text and interface colours are checked against WCAG AA contrast ratios in both light and dark themes
- The site respects your operating system's light/dark and reduced-motion preferences
- Form fields have real labels, and errors are announced to screen readers
- Images carry descriptive alternative text, and decorative images are hidden from assistive technology

## Where we fall short

We test as we build, but we have not yet had this site audited independently, and the Abii mobile app has not been assessed against the mobile accessibility guidelines. Both are planned.
