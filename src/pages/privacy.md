## 1. Information we collect

### Information you give us

- **Email address**, when you join the beta waitlist.
- **Optional survey answer** about how many rooms in your home are the wrong temperature, which we use only to group the waitlist.
- **Correspondence**, if you email us or take part in beta setup or support.

### Information collected by Abii vents and the Abii app

Each Abii vent contains sensors that read:

- **Temperature**
- **Relative humidity**
- **Occupancy**

**Occupancy is detected with a passive infrared (PIR) motion sensor.** Abii vents contain **no camera and no microphone.** They do not record images, video, or audio, and they cannot identify who is in a room. It only detects that motion was detected.

The app also stores the room names and temperature targets you set, and the pairing identifiers of the vents in your home.

## 2. Where your data lives

**Today, sensor and room data stays on your device.** The Abii app stores readings, room configuration, and vent state in local storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to Abii servers.

Your **waitlist email address is the exception**. It is stored with our email provider, Kit (Kit.com), so that we can contact you about the beta.

> **Must be updated before Stage III.** Cloud accounts and cross-device sync are on the roadmap. This section becomes inaccurate the moment sensor data leaves the phone, and this policy has to be revised, and users notified, before that ships.

## 3. Multi-user households

Abii supports more than one person per home, with a household owner who can grant others access to specific rooms.

**Be aware that this means household members can see information about each other.** A person given access to a room can see that room's temperature, humidity, and whether motion has recently been detected there. The household owner can see this for every room, and can see and override changes other members make. Do not grant access to someone you do not want to have that visibility.

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist email until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, and for a short period afterwards in a suppression list so we do not accidentally re-add you. On-device sensor data persists on your phone until you delete the app or clear its data.

- Know what personal information we hold about you
- Get a copy of it
